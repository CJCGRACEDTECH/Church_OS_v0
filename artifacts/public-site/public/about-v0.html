About CJC Church

Centered on Jesus. Open to every nation.

Christ Jesus Centered Church is a place to encounter God, grow through His Word, and build a life of faith in community.

Who we are

One Kingdom. All Nations.

CJC Church serves a multicultural community in Springfield, Virginia. We gather around worship, prayer, biblical teaching, discipleship, and the mission of sharing Jesus Christ.

Whether you are exploring faith, returning to church, or looking for a church home, you are welcome to come as you are and grow with us.

Our leadership

Serving CJC Church

Our leaders are committed to Christ-centered teaching, prayer, discipleship, and caring for the people God has entrusted to this church.

Prophet Yosef Yifru

Senior Pastor of Christ Jesus Centered Church

Prophet Yosef is a Scripture-rooted leader whose ministry reflects the character of Jesus Christ. His ministry is grounded in God’s covenant promise in Exodus 34:10 and a trust that the Lord still works marvelously among His people. Through passages such as Romans 12:2, he calls believers toward transformed minds, steadfast faith, and diligent spiritual growth. His teaching connects biblical truth to the real challenges and joys of everyday life.

Pastor Emanuel King

Pastor of Christ Jesus Centered Church

Pastor Emanuel is a dedicated minister whose life reflects a transforming faith in Jesus Christ. Guided by John 13:34–35, he teaches believers to love one another as a living testimony of discipleship. He emphasizes seeking God’s instruction, allowing His counsel to shape our choices, and walking in righteousness. As the leader of CJC’s English services, he shares the Gospel through biblical wisdom, revelation, and Christ-centered teaching for every area of life.

Our foundation

What we believe

These core beliefs shape how we worship, live, and serve together.

01

Jesus Christ

Jesus is the Son of God, our Savior and Lord, and the center of our faith. We seek to know Him, follow His teaching, and reflect His love in every part of daily life.

02

The Bible

The Bible is the inspired Word of God and our trustworthy foundation for faith and life. Scripture shapes what we believe, how we worship, and the way we live together.

03

Salvation

Salvation is God’s gift of grace, received through faith in Jesus Christ. Through Him we receive forgiveness, new life, and a restored relationship with God.

04

The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit guides, transforms, and empowers believers to follow Christ with wisdom, courage, and a life that increasingly reflects His character.

05

Prayer & Worship

We seek God through prayer and honor Him through gathered worship and lives of obedience. Worship is both our shared expression and our everyday response to God.

06

Our Mission

We are called to share the Gospel, make disciples, love our neighbors, and serve every nation. Our mission extends beyond our gatherings into our community and the world.
